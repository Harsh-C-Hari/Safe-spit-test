ABOUT THE STUDIO

A research studio for questions nobody asked.

SpitLab exists to apply real engineering rigor to ideas with no redeeming value. We model trajectories, we measure impact velocity, we tune drag coefficients — all in service of one outcome: you, spitting on your own screen, and your screen, spitting it right back.

We call this "science for useless ideas." It is the only category of science we currently practice.
