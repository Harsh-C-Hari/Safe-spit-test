THE PRODUCT

One app. One face. Total precision.

The SpitLab app tracks your device's angle, models a full trajectory, and returns your spit to its point of origin. No servers, no cloud sync, no lasting benefit to anyone.

How it works

Three steps. All of them your fault.

01

Aim

Hold your phone at any angle. The app reads your device orientation and calculates a launch vector in real time.

02

Spit

Touch the screen. A fluid model spawns, complete with spin, drag, and a spit type of your choosing.

03

Regret

Face-detection targeting returns the spit to your exact location, at speed, with full confidence in the outcome.
