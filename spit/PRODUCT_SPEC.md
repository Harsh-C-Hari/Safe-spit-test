# PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Core Concept
SAFE//SPIT is a utility app that calculates the exact angle you must point your face/phone out of a moving vehicle to safely spit without the wind blowing it back into your face (or alternatively, to deliberately hit yourself for comedic effect).

## Target Audience
Hackathon judges. The app must be fully functional but inherently satirical. "The joke is the point. The engineering under the joke is not a joke."

## Hackathon Priority System

### MUST HAVE (Core)
- Deterministic spit physics simulation.
- Device orientation tracking (Pitch).
- GPS Speed tracking.
- AR-style HUD with locking reticle.
- Demo Mode (Offline, reliable sequence for judges).

### SHOULD HAVE
- Audio & Haptic feedback on lock.
- Vehicle profiles (Car, Walking, Still).

### NICE TO HAVE
- Real-time multiplayer synchronization.
- ESP32 hardware integration.

### CUT FIRST
- Complex backend logic.
- Cloud profiles.
- Any feature that jeopardizes the offline demo reliability.
